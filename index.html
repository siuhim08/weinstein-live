3. How to read a card

Stage 2A breakout: the stock was in Stage 1 or 4 within the last 60 sessions and closes above its 65-day high, above the 150 and 200-day averages, with positive relative strength. Stage 2 repeat buy: an established Stage 2 stock breaks out of a consolidation of 30 sessions that is no more than 20% deep.

Classic 1988: 2A breakouts only, volume at least 2x the 50-day average, no buys in a Stage 4 market. Stop just under the lower of the 20-day low and the 30-week (150-day) average, then trail the 30-week average.

Modern 2026: volume at least 1.5x, repeat buys allowed. Exit on a close 7% back below the pivot or below the 50-day average, whichever is higher. Target 1 is the nearer of two levels, where one third is trimmed: 20% above the 50-day average, or the measured move. Target 2 is the farther one. Reward/risk uses Target 1.
